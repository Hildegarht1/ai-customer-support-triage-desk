# CV And Portfolio Case Study

## CV Bullet

Built an AI Customer Support Triage Desk using n8n, Telegram, Google Sheets, and LLM prompting to classify inbound customer messages, route tickets by urgency and department, draft knowledge-base-grounded replies, escalate risky cases, and maintain an audit trail.

## Stronger CV Version

Designed and implemented an AI-assisted support operations workflow using self-hosted n8n, Telegram, Google Sheets, and structured LLM outputs. The system receives inbound customer messages, creates ticket records, classifies intent, urgency, sentiment, and department, searches an approved knowledge base, drafts safe replies, escalates low-confidence or high-risk cases, tracks SLA deadlines, and logs workflow events for operational visibility.

## Portfolio Summary

AI Customer Support Triage Desk is a low-code automation project for support teams. It automates first-pass ticket triage while keeping humans in control of risky or customer-facing decisions.

## Problem

Support teams waste time manually reading messages, deciding which department should handle each ticket, and writing repetitive replies. Urgent or frustrated customers can also be missed when there is no structured triage process.

## Solution

I built an automation that receives customer messages, classifies them with AI, searches approved support content, drafts a response, and routes the ticket through approval or escalation based on urgency, confidence, and business risk.
